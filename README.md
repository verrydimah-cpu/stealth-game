**Shadow Ledger** is a top-down stealth game. Night in the Hall of Records: four guards walk their rounds under the lamps, their vision cones sweeping the marble floor. Stay out of the cones, hide in the shadows behind crates, and lift the golden Ledger — then reach the green EXIT without being seen.

Getting spotted raises suspicion: a guard who half-sees you walks over to investigate, and a full alarm sends every guard chasing. Break line of sight and wait the alarm out — you can still recover. Running is fast but loud; holding Shift sneaks quietly. Click anywhere to walk there, or use WASD / arrow keys.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
